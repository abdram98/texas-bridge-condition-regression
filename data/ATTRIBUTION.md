# Data Attribution

The included prepared sample is derived from the **2019 National Bridge
Inventory**, published by the US Department of Transportation's Federal Highway
Administration.

- Official 2019 NBI downloads:
  https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/bridge/nbi/ascii2019.cfm
- NBI dataset catalogue and public-domain designation:
  https://data.transportation.gov/Roadways-and-Bridges/National-Bridge-Inventory-System-NBI-1992/uack-gist
- FHWA Recording and Coding Guide:
  https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/bridge/mtguide.pdf

The US Department of Transportation catalogue identifies the underlying NBI
data as **Public Domain US Government**. The prepared file is a simplified
34,293-record sample rather than the complete 2019 Texas inventory.
